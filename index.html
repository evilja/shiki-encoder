Shiki Encoder Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-10-11

1. Information We Collect

Shiki Encoder (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) collects the following data:

  • Uploaded files: Video files (.mkv, .mp4) and subtitle files (.ass) you provide.
  • Optional metadata: File names or custom titles you specify for encoded outputs.

We do not collect Google account credentials, personal emails, passwords, or any other personal data.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Encoding and processing: Files are processed to produce encoded video outputs.
  • Temporary storage: Files are temporarily stored on our server while processing.

We do not use your files for any purpose other than encoding, and we do not share them with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Uploaded files are stored temporarily and automatically deleted after the encoding process immediately. We do not keep permanent copies unless explicitly specified for testing or debugging purposes.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share your uploaded files or metadata with any external parties. The app may interact with Google APIs (Drive, OAuth) only to facilitate uploading or downloading files you explicitly choose.

5. Your Rights

  • You can revoke access at any time through your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
  • You can request deletion of files or data by contacting us at the email below.
